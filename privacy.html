Star Sums - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Star Sums is a math game for children made by Jason Mock. Contact: support@mocksoftware.com

A copy of this policy is also at https://starsums.mocksoftware.com/privacy.html

What Star Sums remembers

Everything the app remembers - player names or nicknames, grade choices, scores, coins, collection, settings, and the grown-up PIN - stays on this device in the app's local storage and never leaves it. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes it.

The grown-ups area can export a backup file that the grown-up saves themselves. The app never uploads it.

What Star Sums does not do

There are no accounts or sign-in, and no data collection. We do not collect, receive, store, or share any personal information.

There are no ads and no in-app purchases. There is no analytics, crash reporting, third-party SDKs or services, and no location, camera, microphone, contacts, or files access. The app has no internet permission. Fonts and sounds are bundled in the app.

Children

Star Sums is designed so children can play without giving any information. If a family has turned on Android backup, the phone's own backup service may copy app data as part of the phone's normal backup. The developer never sees it.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions? Use the contact above.
